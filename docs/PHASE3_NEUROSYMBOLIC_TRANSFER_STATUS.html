What remains

Do not tune on the consumed ALFWorld formal population. A valid second positive replication needs a new reserve—preferably TIR non-maze with genuine temporally extended tool interventions—and a target-native downside/applicability grounder that models missed commitments, irreversibility, and remaining budget.
